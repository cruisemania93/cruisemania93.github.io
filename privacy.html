1. 수집하는 정보

본 앱은 회원가입 기능이 없으며, 이용자의 개인정보(이메일, 비밀번호, 이름 등)를 직접 수집하지 않습니다.

자동으로 수집되는 정보

앱의 안정성과 품질 향상을 위해 다음 정보가 자동으로 수집될 수 있습니다:

  • 비정상 종료(크래시) 로그 — Firebase Crashlytics
  • 앱 사용 통계 (화면 조회 등) — Firebase Analytics
  • 기기 식별자 — Firebase에서 자동 생성
  • 기기 정보 (OS 버전, 기기 모델)

위 정보는 개인을 식별할 수 없는 익명화된 데이터입니다.

이용자가 직접 입력하는 정보

  • 크루즈 일정 플래너에 입력하는 여행 계획 (기기 내 저장)

  • 앱 비정상 종료 분석 및 안정성 개선

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 17, 2026

CruiseMania ("App") values your privacy and protects personal information in accordance with applicable laws. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

This App does not require account registration and does not directly collect personal information such as email addresses, passwords, or names.

Automatically Collected Information

The following information may be automatically collected to improve app stability and quality:

  • Crash logs — Firebase Crashlytics
  • App usage statistics (screen views, etc.) — Firebase Analytics
  • Device identifiers — automatically generated by Firebase
  • Device information (OS version, device model)

This information is anonymized and cannot be used to identify individuals.

User-Provided Information

  • Travel plans entered in the Trip Planner (stored on device)
  • Cruise experiences recorded in Cruise Collector (stored on device)
  • Email, subject, and message sent through Contact Us

2. How We Use Information

  • Analyzing app crashes and improving stability
  • Analyzing usage patterns and improving service quality
  • Responding to user inquiries

3. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Crash logs: retained for 90 days from collection, then automatically deleted
  • App usage statistics: automatically deleted per Firebase default retention period (14 months)
  • Inquiries: retained for 1 year after resolution, then deleted

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except when required by law.

5. Service Providers

Supabase Inc.Cloud data storage

6. Security Measures

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS)
  • HTTPS-only communication

7. Your Rights

Since this App does not require account registration, there is no need for separate procedures to view, modify, or delete personal information. Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored data.

For inquiries regarding automatically collected analytics data, please contact us at the email below.
