Frequently Asked Questions

What is CruiseMania?

CruiseMania is an all-in-one cruise information platform where you can compare cruise lines and ships, read real passenger reviews, and plan your own cruise itinerary.

Can I book a cruise through the app?

Direct booking is not available at this time. You can book through each cruise line's official website or a travel agency.

How do I write a review?

After logging in, tap the write button in the Reviews tab. Select the ship you traveled on, rate your experience, and write your detailed review.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account section and select 'Delete Account'. All your data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

I forgot my password.

On the login screen, tap 'Forgot your password?' and enter your email. A password reset link will be sent to your email.

Is dark mode supported?

Yes, you can choose between Light, Dark, and System in Settings > Theme.

Which languages are supported?

CruiseMania supports Korean, English, Japanese, and Chinese.
